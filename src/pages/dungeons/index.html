Party Finder Matcher

See the right party at a glance.

Listings that don't fit your class needs, floor or PB requirements are marked, while every listing stays visible and clickable. Nothing is filtered away behind your back.

  • Dungeon friends: who is online, which classes and floors they can cover
  • Your availability: floor, classes and a join PB limit
  • A message template for quick LFG replies

Missing players & requeue

Know before the run goes wrong.

If someone hasn't entered the instance, you get a clear alert before the run gets messy. When a run ends, auto requeue gets you back in without fighting the menus. Both work for dungeon and Kuudra parties.

Reward chests

A slip of the mouse shouldn't cost a drop.

Reroll protection blocks an accidental reroll when a chest holds something valuable. Profit estimates on each chest help you choose which one to open, using current Bazaar and auction prices.

Prices are estimates. An unknown price is shown as unknown, never as zero.

Win screen

A clean summary at the finish.

After a completed run, a readable overlay shows how it went, so you can check the result before you requeue.

Dungeon Routing

A line through the doors to Blood.

Follow a route toward Blood, optionally through starred mobs. By default Blood routing is on only for Mage, Archer and Healer. Thickness, opacity and mob markers are yours to tune.

Healer Crush Camera

The right view for the Storm crush.

Class-specific fixed viewpoints from 26 seconds into Storm until Storm dies. Save viewpoints and check them in the preview, then calibrate before you rely on it in a live run.

Spirit Leap menu

Pick the teammate, not the slot.

A clearer Spirit Leap menu with teammate previews, so you choose who to leap to by what they are doing.

Goldor coordination, F7 and M7 phase 3

Every class knows its next terminal.

A terminal map with each class's job, step-by-step guidance from the gold-standard strategy, and timed pre-leaps. SkyMyce users share short updates in party chat, so the map fills in as your team works.
