Flip finder

Steady, Balanced or Bold.

Pick a risk profile and get ranked flips for your purse. Each card shows the estimated profit after tax, the coins needed and a rough fill time. Details explains every number.

The terminal

Four numbers, never added together.

Purse, coins in buy orders, inventory cost and realized profit, side by side. Realized profit is coins received after tax minus what those items cost, first in, first out. Unsold items and forecasts never count.

  • Flips ranked suggestions
  • Positions what you are flipping and the next step for each
  • History realized profit, ROI and completed sales
  • Replays private recordings of each session
  • More the assistant, alerts, dashboard, export and delete

Bazaar Assistant

One flip, one step at a time.

Buy order, claim, sell offer, claim. A panel beside the menu says what to do next and the button to click glows, but only when there is exactly one match. In chat the expected command appears as ghost text; press Tab to fill it, and mistyped characters turn red.

Sign helper

Green when it's right. You click Done.

On Bazaar signs the expected search, amount or price shows above the sign and turns green once what you typed matches. Enter only moves to the next line; submitting stays your click.

Replays & dashboard

Review a session in your browser.

Every session is recorded privately. Watch it in a dashboard served only on your computer, which shuts down two minutes after you close it. Publishing a replay is always a separate choice, with redaction options.

/sm bazaar dashboard

Opt-in helpers

Two helpers, off by default.

Both are explained before you can turn them on. Hypixel's rules on client modifications apply to you; leave them off if you're unsure.

Auto Command

Press its key (unbound until you set one) to send the one command the current step shows. One press, one command, only with no menu open, and never twice within three seconds. It never runs commands from chat, lore or server messages.

Auto Sign

Types the expected value into an empty Bazaar sign once. You still check it and click Done yourself.
