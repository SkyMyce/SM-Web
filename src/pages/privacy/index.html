Privacy

SkyMyce runs on your computer. Nothing on this page applies until you connect Discord, and the one sharing setting starts anonymous.

Last updated 20 October 2026. This describes the SkyMyce mod and community service as built, and the single sharing setting in the upcoming mod update.

Stays on your computer

These never leave your device, whether or not you connect Discord:

  • Your settings, HUD layout and theme.
  • Dungeon Tracker history, loot, splits and the RNG journal.
  • Bazaar records, positions, profit history and session replays. Publishing a replay is a separate, explicit choice with redaction options.
  • Daily Digest history.

They live in config/skymyce in your instance folder. Some features read public game data, such as auction and market prices and SkyBlock news, without sending anything about you.

Community data sharing

After you connect Discord, one setting in /sm community decides what you contribute to the community.

On

Shares dungeon stats, RNG drops, activity and leaderboard contributions, and shows your Minecraft name on public reports.

On (anon) Default

Shares the same categories, but your name appears as "Anonymous player". Anonymous entries can't be found by searching your name.

Off

Stops all sharing. Your earlier contributions are removed, and RNG reports already posted are suppressed and deleted from the feed.

Turning sharing back on starts from that moment. Nothing from the time it was off is sent later. Chat and cosmetics are separate features with their own identities and are not affected by this setting.

What each category contains

Dungeon stats

What
Catacombs and class levels, completed floors, completion and S+ times and your selected class, used by Party Finder tools so friends can see what you can run. New completed-run records, estimated chest profit and run times for leaderboards.
Who sees it
Friends' clients see the cached stats; leaderboards show totals per Minecraft account, by name only when sharing is On.
Kept
Friend-cache stats for about ten minutes. Leaderboard records until you turn sharing off or storage limits retire them.

RNG drops

What
Newly detected supported drops: item, activity (dungeon, slayer, Kuudra or fishing), time and, when seen, rarity, display name and floor. Never your inventory.
Who sees it
The community RNG feed in game and the SkyMyce Discord's RNG channel, and the RNG leaderboard. Your name only when sharing is On.
Kept
Feed posts for about a week in game; counted for leaderboards until you turn sharing off.

Activity

What
Reserved for supported progress contributions. No activity publisher exists today, so nothing is collected in this category.
Who sees it
Nobody yet.

Leaderboards

What
Rankings built from the dungeon and RNG records above. Values are unverified client observations; coin values are estimates.
Who sees it
Players in game, the Discord bot and this website. Each Minecraft account is ranked separately.

When you connect Discord

Identity

What
Your Discord ID, username and display name (the identify scope only), the Minecraft account (UUID and name) you connected, and your SkyMyce version. Your game proves the Minecraft account to the relay; your password and Microsoft login are never sent.
Why
To link community features to one person and to enforce bans on abuse.

Session

What
Short-lived access tokens (15 minutes) and a refresh token (up to 90 days), stored by the relay as hashes, plus connection times and an audit log of account actions.

Device observation

What
When you connect or refresh a session, the mod sends a one-way SHA-256 hash of an existing operating-system identifier (for example the SMBIOS UUID on Windows), with its source and your platform (Windows, macOS, Linux or other). The identifier itself is hashed on your computer and never sent.
Why
Private administrator metadata for investigating abuse. It is never used to grant or block access, and a missing or changed value does not limit your connection.

Rate limiting

What
Your IP address is used transiently by the relay's rate limits on Cloudflare; repeated abuse can trigger a temporary cooldown on your account.

Features that send data when you use them

Global Chat

What
Your messages, replies and reactions, with your linked Minecraft name. Links to images or video are previewed by recipients.
Who sees it
Connected SkyMyce players and the SkyMyce Discord's global channel. Discord keeps bridged messages under Discord's own policies.
Kept
Chat is live only. The relay holds a message for up to two minutes for delivery, and a short preview (up to 256 characters) for 15 minutes so replies, edits and reactions work.

Private messages

What
Messages you send with /sm msg, delivered live to the recipient.

Shared cosmetics

What
Only when you share: your display name and its styling, player and nametag sizes, and a cape preset or uploaded 64 × 32 PNG.
Who sees it
SkyMyce players' clients and the website gallery. Uploaded capes are stored privately and checked before use.
Remove
Reset your look or delete capes in /sm cosmetics.

Updates and alerts

What
Checking for a signed update downloads the release manifest and file.

Opt out, disconnect, delete

  • Stop sharing: set Community data sharing to Off in /sm community. Prior contributions are removed or suppressed.
  • Stay anonymous: keep the default, On (anon). Switching from On to On (anon) removes your earlier contributions, so named reports don't linger.
  • Leave chat or cosmetics: stop using Global Chat; reset your look or delete capes in the studio.
  • Disconnect: use Disconnect in /sm community, or the SkyMyce Discord bot's account commands to disconnect or unlink a Minecraft account.
  • Delete your account record: after turning sharing off and disconnecting, ask the team to remove the remaining account and audit records. Contact the team through the SkyMyce Discord.
  • Remove local data: delete config/skymyce from your instance folder.

This website

  • No cookies, analytics, ads or third-party scripts or fonts. It is a static site hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.
  • Website sign-in, when enabled, keeps its token in this tab's session storage only and sends it only to the SkyMyce relay.
  • The preview session and demo data on community pages never leave your browser.
