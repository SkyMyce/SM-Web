Does SkyMyce play or trade for me?

No. It reads menus, chat and what your client can see, and tells you what to do next. Clicks, trades, confirmations and movement are yours.

Is it allowed on Hypixel?

SkyMyce is not affiliated with Hypixel or Mojang. You are responsible for following Hypixel's rules on client modifications. If you are unsure about a feature, leave it off.

Do I need Discord?

No. Every local feature works without it. Discord is only needed for Global Chat, community leaderboards, sharing cosmetics and signed updates.

What data does it share?

No community data is shared until you connect Discord. After that, one setting, Community data sharing, decides: On, On (anon) (the default) or Off. See Privacy for every category.

Where is my data stored?

Settings and local history live in config/skymyce in your instance folder.

How do updates work?

Automatic updates are off by default. Update Now in /sm community stages a signed release; restart Minecraft to apply it. You can always download from the download page instead.

Which mods do I need with it?

Fabric Loader and Fabric Language Kotlin. Fabric API and SkyblockPV are bundled. See Install & troubleshooting.

Why are the website's leaderboards and chat marked "Preview data"?

The live service doesn't serve the website yet. Until it does, those pages show deterministic demo data with a clear badge, so you can see how they work. The data in game is live.

Where do I report a bug?

On GitHub Issues. Include the SkyMyce version, Minecraft version and the relevant part of logs/latest.log, with account names and tokens removed.
