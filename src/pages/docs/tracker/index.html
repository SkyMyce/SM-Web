Dungeon Tracker

Turn on Dungeons → Runs → Dungeon Tracker, then open it with:

/sm dungeon

It shows run history, loot records and estimated rewards. Filter by floor, mode and dates, and step through the timeline of each run.

  • Profit is an estimate from current prices. If a price is missing, the total is marked incomplete rather than counting the item as zero.
  • Rates per hour only use runs with matched start and end times, and say how many runs matched.
  • Chests include rerolls, Hunting Box shards and master stars.

Tracker widget

Add the Dungeon Tracker widget in /sm hud. It shows time, runs per hour, chests and rerolls, profit, Catacombs XP per hour, and your progress to a target Catacombs level as XP left and an estimated number of runs.

Levels

The Levels tab shows Catacombs and class progress toward a target you choose, as runs, XP or a percentage.

Dungeon Splits

Live splits for each phase of a run, your personal bests, the best possible time and averages per floor. Open the splits view with:

/sm splits

Move the splits widget with /sm hud; its background opacity is adjustable.

RNG journal

Your preserved personal acquisitions with dates, counts and measured history. Search by item and filter by period:

/sm journal

The journal is personal and local. Saved history is never uploaded automatically.

The Recent RNG widget keeps your latest drop and its time on screen. Move it with /sm hud.

Journal comparison

With a community connection and Community data sharing on, the journal can compare your shared RNG drops with the community for the same period: number of participants, total drops and the mean per identity. Only consenting SkyMyce players are counted, which the comparison states. If sharing is off, your own count is shown as unavailable.

Leaderboards

Community rankings by category and period are in game with /sm leaderboard and on the leaderboards page. They are built only from consenting players' shared, unverified client observations.
