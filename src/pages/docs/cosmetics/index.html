Display name

Type plain text, or paste Minecraft text-component JSON for colours and styles. Names are limited to keep them readable and safe:

  • Up to 32 visible characters, not blank and without leading or trailing spaces.
  • Letters, numbers, common symbols and simple separators.
  • Colours by Minecraft name (for example aqua or gold) or as #RRGGBB.
  • Bold, italic, underlined and strikethrough. No click events, fonts or translations.
[{"text":"Sky","color":"aqua","bold":true},{"text":"Rat","color":"#b59be6"}]

Size

Scale a player between 0.1 and 3.0, separately for width, height and depth if you want. The nametag has its own scale from 0.5 to 2.0.

Capes

Pick one of four presets (SkyMyce, Forest, Sunset, Void) or upload your own:

  • PNG, exactly 64 × 32 pixels, up to 256 KiB.
  • Up to 8 custom capes, 2 MiB in total.
  • Uploads are checked before they are stored; animated or malformed PNGs are rejected.

Sharing

To let other SkyMyce players see your look, connect Discord (Community & Discord) and share from the studio. /sm cosmetics link opens the linking step directly. Shared cosmetics are public rendering data: your chosen name, sizes and cape. Your real IGN stays authoritative everywhere else.
