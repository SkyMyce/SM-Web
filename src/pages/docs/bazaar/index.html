Your first flip

  1. Join a SkyBlock island and make sure your purse shows on the scoreboard.
  2. Run /sm bazaar (or open Economy → Bazaar Trading) and click Start flipping.
  3. In Flips, pick Steady, Balanced or Bold. Each card shows the estimated profit after tax, the coins needed and a rough fill time. Details explains the numbers.
  4. Click Guide this flip. The Bazaar search opens, the suggested amount is copied and the Bazaar Assistant starts. Place the buy order yourself.
  5. Follow the Next step banner: claim the bought items, create a sell offer at the suggested price, then claim your coins.
  6. Click Stop flipping, or run /sm bazaar off, when you're done. Leaving the island also stops the session.

SkyMyce reads Bazaar chat receipts (order set up, filled, claimed, cancelled) and the native Manage Orders menu. If something looks out of date, use Sync orders.

The terminal

Four tiles show Purse (coins you can spend now), coins in buy orders, the inventory cost of items you hold and realized profit. They are never added together.

Bazaar Assistant

The assistant walks you through one flip at a time: buy order, claim, sell offer, claim. It never clicks, confirms or places anything.

  • Start with Guide this flip, More → Guide the best flip, /sm bazaar assist or the ] key. Stop with [ or /sm bazaar assist stop. Both keys also work inside menus; change them in Options → Controls → Miscellaneous.
  • The panel says what to do next. In menus it sits beside the menu; drag it anywhere to keep it there.
  • The highlight marks the button to click only when exactly one slot matches and still shows that button. Otherwise nothing is highlighted and the panel explains why.
  • In chat the expected command appears as faint ghost text and anything typed wrong turns red. Press Tab to fill it in. Other commands keep normal Tab completion.
  • On signs the expected search, amount or price shows above the sign and turns green once it matches. Then click Done; Enter only moves to the next line.
  • Price entry chooses between matching the order book (Top Order +0.1 / Best Offer -0.1) and typing the recommended price.
  • Profit pop-ups rise from the order you clicked when a guided sale is claimed, and the panel shows this run's profit per hour from the same records as History.

Two helpers, off by default

  • Auto Command sends the shown command when you press its key (unbound until you set it): one press, one command, only with no menu open, and never twice within three seconds.
  • Auto Sign pre-types the expected value into an empty Bazaar sign. You still check it and click Done.

Your responsibility. Hypixel's rules on client modifications apply to you. Leave both helpers off if you're unsure.

Profit and records

  • Realized profit = coins received after tax − what those items cost, first in, first out. Unsold items and forecasts never count.
  • A filled sale counts once you claim its coins; until then it shows as waiting under Positions.
  • Needs your review lists receipts that couldn't be matched with certainty. Sync orders usually fixes them. Fix record saves a personal correction.
  • The relist warning appears on the seventh relist of the same position by default.

HUD, dashboard and alerts

  • The Bazaar Active Flip HUD appears while flipping and shows the position that needs you most. Cycle positions with its key or /sm bazaar cycle.
  • /sm bazaar dashboard opens a private page served only on this computer. It shuts down two minutes after you close or hide it, or with /sm bazaar close-dashboard.
  • Exceptional-flip alerts (in game, dashboard, Discord DM, personal webhook) only fire while flipping with live data, for flips you can afford.

Privacy

Your trading history stays on this device. Publishing a replay is always a separate choice with redaction options, and revoking a link or deleting local history never changes the other.
