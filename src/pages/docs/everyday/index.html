Daily Digest

A daily snapshot of news, event updates, mayor information and your personal dailies, with optional community RNG reports. Open it with:

/sm digest

Personal Digest history stays local.

Auto Refill

Under Utils → Inventory → Auto Refill, choose which consumable items refill from your sack when a stack runs out.

Stash value filter

Economy → Item Values → Stash Value Filter filters low-value stash items using current prices and prevents unwanted pickups.

Loadout keybinds

Combat → Gear → Loadout Keybinds swaps equipment loadouts with keys you choose, with safeguards in the loadout menu.

Status bars

Separate, animated health, mana, defense, speed, vitality and respiration bars. They are off by default; turn them on under Combat → Player → Status Bars, then arrange them:

/sm bars

The editor anchors bars around the hotbar, snaps them to guides, and sets size, colour and per-bar options such as effective health on the defense bar.

Overlays

  • NPC Dialogue shows NPC lines in a dialogue box with the speaker's portrait; pick options with number keys.
  • Information Overlays show client details and skill XP progress, with an optional progress bar.
  • Ability Cooldowns show the held item's ability timer and a ready notification.
  • Sign Enter lets you press Enter to finish Auction House, Bazaar and other signs.

Price history

Hover an item in a menu and press P for its Bazaar or auction price graph.

Auction browser

Search cached auction listings with filters, bids and item lore:

/sm ah

Social and other helpers

  • Party Commands: choose party chat commands and cooldowns, with an optional stats API fallback.
  • Player Utilities: cached friend activity, locations and profile information.
  • Crystal Hollows Helpers: lobby information and configurable nucleus warp timing.
  • Bal Notifications: share Bal spawn and health milestones with your party.

Theme and HUD

Pick a theme preset and a graphics mode under Settings → Appearance; see Graphics & performance. Move, scale, anchor and reset widgets with /sm hud.
