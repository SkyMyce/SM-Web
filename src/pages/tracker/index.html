Tracker

Every run, every drop, kept.

Run history, loot and personal RNG live in one place on your computer. Filter by floor, mode and date, and see how far you are from the next level.

Dungeon Tracker

Your history, without the digging.

Review runs by floor and mode, with dates, timelines and estimated rewards. Profit is an estimate from current prices; incomplete data is marked rather than counted as zero.

/sm dungeon

Loot history

Chests and rerolls, run by run.

Each recorded chest keeps what was inside and its estimated value, including chests with shards and master stars.

RNG journal

Your rare drops, searchable.

Preserved personal acquisitions with dates, counts and measured history. Search an item, filter by period, and keep the record even if you never connect to the community.

/sm journal

Levels

How far to the next level, in runs.

The Levels tab shows Catacombs and class progress toward a target you choose, as runs, XP or a percentage. The tracker widget can show the same target while you play.

Tracker widget

The numbers that matter, on screen.

Time, runs per hour, chests, rerolls, profit and Catacombs XP per hour. Rates only use runs with matched times, and the widget says when they don't.

Dungeon Splits

Live splits, with your best beside them.

Split times through each phase, personal bests, the best possible time and averages per floor, with server time and lag shown separately.

/sm splits

Journal comparison

Your drops next to the community's.

With a community connection, compare your shared RNG drops with the mean across consenting SkyMyce players for the same period. Only players who share are counted, and the comparison says so.

Optional. Needs Discord linking and Community data sharing turned on.
