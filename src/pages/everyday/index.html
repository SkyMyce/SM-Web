Everyday QoL

The small things, handled.

Dailies in one place, stacks that refill, bars you can arrange and menus that answer to Enter. Each one is a switch in /sm.

Daily Digest

Your day in SkyBlock, on one page.

News, event updates, mayor information and your personal dailies, with optional community RNG reports. Personal history stays local.

/sm digest

Auto Refill

Stacks that top themselves up.

Choose which consumables refill from your sack when they run out, so grinding and runs aren't interrupted by trips to the sack menu.

Stash value filter

Skip the junk in your stash.

Low-value stash items are filtered using current prices, and unwanted pickups are prevented, so sorting a stash takes seconds.

Loadout keybinds

Swap builds with a key.

Bind equipment loadouts to keys, with safeguards in the loadout menu so a stray press doesn't change the wrong slot.

Status bars, off by default

6 bars, where you want them.

Separate animated health, mana, defense, speed, vitality and respiration bars. Arrange them with /sm bars: anchors around the hotbar, snapping, sizes, colours and per-bar options.

Overlays

Readable by default.

  • NPC dialogue box with the speaker and numbered options you pick with number keys
  • Skill XP widget with a progress bar instead of action-bar spam
  • Ability cooldown for your held item, with a ready notification
  • Sign Enter to finish Auction House, Bazaar and bank signs
  • Client info in a compact widget

Price history & auctions

Hover, press P, see the trend.

Hover an item in any menu and press P for its Bazaar or auction price graph. The auction browser searches cached listings with filters, bids and item lore.

/sm ah

Appearance

Pick a theme. Pick a graphics mode.

Every menu and HUD widget follows one theme preset. Move and scale widgets with /sm hud.

  • Dark glass
  • Minimal
  • Borealis
  • Emberforge
  • Verdant
  • High contrast
  • Light

Extremely Light

The lightest menus, for the slowest machines.

Light

Fewer effects, simple transitions.

Medium

A balance of polish and speed.

High

Full effects and GUI animations.

Graphics mode also controls GUI animations. It only changes SkyMyce's own menus: HUD information, tracking, routes and the Crush Camera are never reduced, and Minecraft's settings are not touched.
