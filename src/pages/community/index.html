How linking works

In game, run /sm community and connect Discord. That link is what lets you chat, appear on leaderboards and share cosmetics. One setting, Community data sharing, controls what you contribute: On, On (anon) by default, or Off.

Signing in on this website will use the same Discord account through the SkyMyce relay. It is not live yet; until it is, community pages show labelled preview data.
