How it behaves

Quiet by design.

Local first

Dungeon tools, tracking, Bazaar records and the HUD run on your computer. No Discord account needed.

Guides, never clicks

SkyMyce reads menus and chat and tells you what to do next. Trades, clicks and confirmations stay yours.

Optional community

Global Chat, leaderboards and cosmetics sharing are opt-in through Discord. One setting controls data sharing.

Signed updates

Releases are signed with Ed25519 and checked before install. Automatic updates are off by default.
