SkyMyce is a client-side Fabric mod for Hypixel SkyBlock: dungeon tools, run tracking and a guided Bazaar workflow in a HUD you arrange yourself. It says what's next and leaves every click to you.

Four areas. Turn on only what you use.

Every feature is a switch in the /sm menu, and every one of them runs on your computer. None of them needs an account.

  • DungeonsFrom the Party Finder to Goldor.Party matching, missing-player alerts, chest reroll protection, routing, the Crush Camera and per-class Goldor steps./sm pf
  • TrackerEvery run, every drop, kept.Run history, loot, an RNG journal, live splits and Catacombs and class progress toward the level you choose./sm dungeon
  • BazaarGuided flips. Your clicks.Ranked flip ideas, a private profit ledger and an assistant that points at the next button. It never trades for you./sm bazaar
  • EverydayThe small things, handled.Daily Digest, Auto Refill, stash filtering, loadout keys, status bars, overlays and theme presets./sm digest

Quiet by design.

SkyMyce reads menus and chat and tells you what matters. It does not click, trade or send anything for you.

Local first

Dungeon tools, tracking, Bazaar records and the HUD run on your computer. No Discord account needed.

Guides, never clicks

The Bazaar Assistant highlights one button and says why. Placing, claiming and confirming stay yours.

Optional community

Global Chat, leaderboards and cosmetics sharing are opt-in through Discord. One setting controls what you share.

Signed updates

Releases are signed with Ed25519 and checked before install. Automatic updates are off by default.

One JAR in your mods folder.

Install it next to Fabric Language Kotlin, launch with the Fabric profile and type /sm.
